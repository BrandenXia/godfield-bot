# ADR 0053: Official Training schema-v10 adapter readiness gate

## Status

Accepted, 2026-09-27.

## Context

ADR 0052 introduced a passive schema-v10 bridge but deliberately withheld live
control. A prospective collection subsequently produced 29 official-CPU runs:
28 completed games, one progressing game stopped by the former 100-click
ceiling, 1,479 shadow samples, and 981 executable behavior decisions. The
heuristic won 2 of the 28 completed games. Those outcomes belong to the
heuristic and cannot measure the shadow candidate's counterfactual performance.

Naive aggregate coverage was 90.62%, but 89 of the 92 abstentions were the
intended response to Fog hiding the opponent row. Treating those observations
as adapter failures would encourage unsafe use of stale hidden values. Among
892 decisions with an exact visible representation, the candidate covered 889
(99.66%); the three gaps were selectable cards beyond the nine-slot input.

The cohort includes 123 exact-view illness opportunities, 53 Flash, 161 Dark
Cloud, and 46 Dream. It also exercises all ten reviewed neural action families.
The candidate agreed with 817 of 889 exact-view behavior decisions. Agreement
is diagnostic only: disagreement may be the learned policy's intended
improvement over the weak heuristic.

## Decision

Add `official-training-shadow-readiness-v1`, generated by `models
evaluate-training-shadow-readiness`. The command discovers immutable native
reports for the exact candidate and audits all matching official Training runs.
It writes an owner-only report containing source run IDs, native evaluation
IDs, thresholds, metrics, failure reasons, and a SHA-256 digest over every
accepted report, run, and event.

The gate validates:

- candidate status, lineage, weights, client, vocabulary, schema-v10
  architecture, and strict Dream ruleset;
- three independent passing native evaluations with the frozen parent and
  Dream heuristic matchups;
- run-level shadow policy, model, weight, schema, client, and behavior identity;
- exact observation/state/legal-action/decision/evidence event adjacency;
- state digests, legal membership, model identity, coverage/abstention shape,
  scores, and agreement flags;
- classified terminal state, outcome, sparse reward, and completed-run summary
  consistency.

Visibility-hidden decisions form a separate fail-closed stratum. They must
abstain for the exact visibility reason and do not enter exact-view coverage.
All other executable decisions do enter that denominator.

The default thresholds require:

- three passing native evaluations;
- 20 completed official games and a 0.80 Wilson lower bound on completion;
- 500 exact-view opportunities and a 0.98 Wilson lower bound on coverage;
- at most five exact-view encoding gaps under the original v1 gate;
- at least 20 illness, 10 Flash, 20 Dark Cloud, and 20 Dream opportunities;
- at least 20 verified hidden-visibility abstentions;
- at least one proposal in each reviewed action family;
- no evidence-integrity errors or failed matching runs.

Behavior agreement and behavior win/loss counts remain visible but are not gate
criteria. Operational aborts are also reported; completion confidence captures
their effect. The observed action-limit abort exposed a harness ceiling rather
than a stalled game, so official Training now permits up to 1,000 clicks and
defaults continuous campaigns to 300. The independent no-progress timer remains
bounded and continues to stop actual deadlocks.

## Result

Candidate `df08842c-f5e8-4317-8721-0245310c92ac`, weights SHA-256
`471b096142e7468d9e7ff8189aa5c8490927cfefa5d103c57a32f362ef4e522c`,
passed the new gate over the 29-run cohort. The report measured a 0.8282
completion lower bound, 0.9902 exact-view coverage lower bound, zero integrity
errors, and complete required status/action-family representation. Immutable
local report `da85614f-695e-4045-93c6-b5839741d6d0` records the full input
digest and source identities.

ADR 0056 supersedes the absolute five-gap condition for new reports. The v2
gate retains gaps in the strict 98% Wilson coverage calculation but is monotonic
as the prospective cohort grows. Existing v1 reports remain valid for their
exact immutable inputs.

## Safety boundary

Passing means the schema-v10 adapter has enough prospective evidence to design
a guarded intervention trial. It does not authorize candidate clicks, establish
candidate win rate, make the model promotion-eligible, or justify filling Fog
with stale information. A separate decision must define intervention scope,
rollback conditions, per-game attribution, and explicit operator authorization
before candidate-controlled schema-v10 games begin.
