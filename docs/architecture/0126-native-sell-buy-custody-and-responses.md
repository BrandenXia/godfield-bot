## Phase choices and private custody

Ready Buy reserves its displayed leader and enters ordinary phase3 targets.
Ready Sell opens phase6 mode2: slot+1 chooses/toggles one displayed owned unused
parcel;0 confirms that selection, capacity+1 cancels without use or tick.
After64 edits no new toggles occur; cancel always remains available. Only the
owner's public displayed hand/performed flags affect selection. Target selection
does not inspect actual leader/parcel identities. Cross-role hidden leaders fail
atomically on cast; they aren't disclosed early or silently approximated.

Cast consumes the actual trade leader once. Sell's actual eligible parcel leaves
the hand into private owned custody, not consumption, performance or replacement
gift. Family7/origin6 has ATK0. The existing shared response code handles genuine
Super Mirror chains; parcel custody persists across hops while the current source
changes. Numeric armor, counters, filters and weapon/miracle-only special effects
are not legal trade responses. Original turn ownership/tick and deferred receipts
remain unchanged across every reflection. Source-pinned normal choices don't
produce block/bounce trade paths; generic recovery remains fail-closed for future
producers, not a fabricated live ability.

For Buy, after the final defensive response, one actual unused target card is
uniformly sampled and reserved. Performed miracles are excluded, not treated as
normal cards; empty eligible hands resolve without a false prompt. The item and
price are revealed in phase6 mode3, and actor becomes the **current** source.
0 refuses and1 purchases. A reflected Buy therefore asks the reflector to buy
from the current target, not the original caster or final defender.

Private custody prevents a sold/offered Super Mirror, Sun or Bow from remaining
simultaneously usable in its old hand. Rejected purchases return the exact saved
ID/model/disguise/unused flag to the original owner's tail, with no payment/gift.
Accepted transfers create a fresh monotonic native ID, reset the unused disguise
according to receiver Dream and use the explicit configured hand-cap/oldest
overflow policy. Neither transfers nor refunds produce replacement gifts.
Native reject-overflow fixtures fail the entire batch; opt-in oldest eviction
is tracked separately. Custody, tail return, new IDs/disguises, overflow and
uniform selection remain provisional server rules.

## Wallet and reaction ordering: explicitly provisional

For Sell the current target pays, and the current source receives credit.
For Buy the current source pays, and the current target receives credit.
The recipient receives the item first, then both wallets settle at the actual
pinned price through shared CP→MP→HP debit and capped requested-price credit.
Only then do actual owned Sun/final-death/Bow hooks run, before the original
owner's single tick and ending checks. Thus a newly received Sun or Bow may
participate in payment death locally. That order is **not official-verified**.
Unpaid amount and lost capped credit are recorded rather than fabricated as
successful debit; requested credit is still granted locally. Existing cap100
is retained explicitly, not confused with client99 or silently migrated.

Payment HP loss uses the existing payment cause3 and guardian-HP-debit lifecycle,
not numeric incoming attack damage, Darkness finishing, absorption or counters.
New post-death shots use the shared depth-first queue; no extra owner tick is
introduced. All wallet/card/custody/RNG/queue/status/counter mutations stage
before whole-batch commit. Late stale/duplicate/hidden/overflow rows cannot
partially consume earlier parcels, generate new IDs, refake, pay, revive or tick.

Native confused-ready turns sample valid actual Sell parcels/targets and the
original buyer's purchase decision without new policy selection prompts.
The original forced command has policy weight0; ordinary defensive responses
and a new reflecting buyer remain voluntary. Ready-only control/sampling remain
provisional, consistent with the approved controller boundary. The exact staged
illness-seed XOR streams are target `0xba7c9045f12c7f99`, offer
`0x24a19947b3916cf7`, received disguise `0x0801f2e2858efc16`, forced purchase
`0x636920d871574e69`, forced sale parcel `0xa1f1651d92b39463`.

## Observable boundaries and conservation

`market_observations[B,9]`: active,kind,stage,current source,target,selected sale
choice,revealed model,revealed price,edit count. Kinds1/2 mean Sell/Buy; stages
1/2/3/4 mean sale selection,target selection,response,purchase. Future Buy
victims, random streams, fake originals and private custody IDs are never policy
features. The seven existing trade fields distinguish modes1/2/3; purchase mode
does not expose the original owner's private consumed-card slot.

`market_snapshot[B,25]` contains reservations,edits,casts,draws,redirects,
resolutions,offers,empty offers,purchases/refusals,transfers/refunds,payment and
credit/cap/overflow counters. Trusted `market_escrow_snapshot[B,6]` is original
owner,instance,actual model,fake model,performed,present. It is diagnostic only.
Custody is represented even at decision truncation, not silently dropped or
reported as completed trade; reset clears it. Views are owned readonly snapshots.
Queue payloads own custody across reservation/response and preserve shared
enqueued=resolved+suppressed+queued+active accounting. Preserving command1 is
not proof of neural action/observation/checkpoint compatibility.
